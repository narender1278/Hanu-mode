Acme Technologies Employee Handbook (sample document for testing)

Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 1.5 days per month. Up to 8 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; any extra days expire on 31 March.

Sick Leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if the absence lasts more than 2 consecutive days.

Work From Home
The company follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, and may work from home on Monday and Friday. Exceptions need written approval from the reporting manager.

Reimbursements
Internet expenses up to 1,000 rupees per month are reimbursable with a bill. Travel for client visits is reimbursed at actuals, with a limit of 3,000 rupees per day for hotel stays.

Notice Period
The standard notice period is 60 days. It can be shortened to 30 days with HR and manager approval.
